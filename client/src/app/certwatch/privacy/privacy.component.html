CertWatch

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Overview

CertWatch is an iOS app that helps you monitor SSL/TLS certificate expiry for hostnames you choose. Matthew Frankland ("we", "us") does not operate a backend service for CertWatch.

Data stored on your device

Endpoints, certificate details, tags, notes, and app settings are stored locally on your device using SwiftData in an App Group container shared with the CertWatch widget extension.

Network activity

When you add or refresh an endpoint, CertWatch connects directly to the hostname and port you enter to perform a TLS handshake and read certificate metadata. We do not receive or store that data on our servers.

Notifications

Expiry alerts are scheduled locally on your device. CertWatch does not use push notification servers.

Purchases

CertWatch Pro is sold as a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple via StoreKit. We do not collect payment details.

Analytics and tracking

CertWatch does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking.
