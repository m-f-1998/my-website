iOS App

CertWatch

Local-first SSL/TLS certificate expiry tracker for iPhone and iPad. Monitor domains you care about, inspect certificate chains, get local expiry alerts, and view status on Home Screen widgets.

Features

  • Monitor up to 5 endpoints on the free tier
  • Live TLS certificate inspection for hostnames you enter
  • Expiry dashboard with status badges and certificate detail
  • Local notifications before certificates expire
  • CertWatch Pro: unlimited endpoints, widgets, custom alerts, export/import, and background refresh
