iOS App

Recall Watch

UK food and product recalls, matched to what you actually buy. Official FSA and OPSS notices, on your iPhone and iPad, with no account.

Features

  • Browse consumer food and product alerts from the Food Standards Agency and OPSS
  • Match alerts to allergens, brands, keywords, categories, and saved products
  • Free tier: three watchlist items and match notifications
  • Recall Watch Pro: unlimited watchlist, 90-day history, widgets, quiet hours, background refresh, and export

Sources

Alert text is public sector information from the Food Standards Agency and OPSS, reused under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
