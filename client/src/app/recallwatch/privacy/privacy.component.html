Recall Watch

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Overview

Recall Watch is an iOS app that matches official UK food and product recall notices to a watchlist you choose. Matthew Frankland ("we", "us") does not operate a backend service for Recall Watch and does not create user accounts.

Data stored on your device

Your watchlist, saved alerts, checked and not-mine flags, and settings are stored locally on your device using SwiftData in an App Group container shared with the Recall Watch widget extension.

Network activity

When the app refreshes, it fetches public feeds from the Food Standards Agency and OPSS / GOV.UK. We do not receive that traffic. Alert text is reused under the Open Government Licence.

Notifications

Match alerts are scheduled locally on your device. Recall Watch does not use push notification servers.

Purchases

Recall Watch Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple via StoreKit. We do not collect payment details.

Analytics and tracking

Recall Watch does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking.

Medical and legal

Recall Watch is not medical or legal advice and does not diagnose allergies. Always follow the official government notice.
